# Ruling audit, 2026-09-18: consolidated findings of five independent refuters

Scope: the ruling of record in ch09_tolerance.tex (sec:tolerance:record, as read 2026-09-18), the chapter opening, FRAME_ANALYSIS_PREDECLARATION.md (amendments 1 to 7), CADENCE_REPORT.md, table_of_record.csv, robustness_credit.csv, bao_baseline_preview.csv and gain_bao.csv, the residual.py and residual_templates.py docstrings, and METHODS_NOTES sections 15, 20, 22 and 23. ## 0. The one fact the refuters did not have

All five refuters treat the shortest-baseline basis as an open blocker. It is not open: amendment 7 of the predeclaration (2026-09-18, 03:49 UTC, after the audit checks) concedes it in the author's own words ("the rule's own logic, that an exclusion must survive every credit the data can give, requires the exclusion to be read there") and bao_baseline_preview.csv / gain_bao.csv implement it as a preview. On that basis 27 keeps (-8.8 dB), 29 is marginal (+2.2 dB), 14 is undetermined (-1.5 dB, east-west coherence refused in every class), and the other nineteen remain excised by 9.0 dB (28) to 37.1 dB (30). The manuscript's record section predates the amendment and still rules on the 0.3 m class; every sentence quoted by the refuters against that basis ("the conservative choice", "a question for the forecast, not for the capture") is now contradicted by the author's own predeclaration. That is the first thing to fix, and it is mechanical.

What the refuters could not have seen, and what this audit adds, is that the basis change moves the decisive problem rather than removing it: on the east-west classes the no-transmitter reference channel reads the same as seventeen of the twenty-two excised channels. Finding S1 below is therefore the blocker that survives amendment 7.

### S3 (blocker on the framing, not on any row): what "excised" means, and what the collaboration will read into it

Raised by scope 4, consistency 6, tolerance-model 5, measurement 2. At G = 1 every channel passes with the cut (largest 0.63, channel 30). The whole 14 to 50 dB is the coherence factor G = tau_c / T_frame under a model residual.py calls "a declared screening model, not the general result". The property that makes G large (level steady over an hour or more; excess phasor coherent at 0.98 to 1.00 over a sidereal day on the 0.3 m class) is the property under which the author's own model says the residual "is overwhelmingly m = 0 and is removed by the standard ground filter" (term 3), and the sidereal-day cap kept in the "no credit" world exists, by the docstring's own words, "to keep the ground filter and the coherence factor from pricing the same power twice". So the no-credit world is not credit-free, and "Nothing in the table rests on a credit the forecast cannot represent" (section 22) is false in letter.

What is established: no frame-level mask substitutes for removing a steady sidereal-stationary term; on the shortest baselines that term is 14 to 50 dB over a one-sigma bias tolerance under a noise-shaped template; on the east-west classes it is measured above the estimator floor on 17, 30, 31, 33 and 35 and is 14 to 37 dB over there.

What is not established: that the residual after CHIME's per-day sidereal-mean or m-mode removal, on BAO-carrying baselines, exceeds tolerance (unpriced by the rule's own choice); that the cross-correlation analyses the collaboration has published are affected (a residual uncorrelated with the galaxy field is a variance term there, and the manuscript never names the estimator its bias tolerance presumes); that a per-channel residual-amplitude nuisance, marginalised in the BAO fit, or inverse-variance down-weighting, fails (neither is evaluated; the tolerance is a fixed-template bias); that other science with these bins (FRB, pulsar) is touched.

What answers it (all sentences, no analysis):

- Replace the closing claim with: "The table of record excises a channel when no frame-level masking policy brings its residual within a one-sigma bias tolerance at the measured persistence, with the 200 ns cut credited and no credit for the sidereal-mean or m = 0 removal that the standard pipeline applies to exactly such a term. It therefore bounds what masking can do; it does not price what the pipeline leaves, and it says nothing about these bins' use for FRB, pulsar or cross-correlation science."
- In sec:fisher and sec:record: "The tolerance is a bias tolerance for the auto-power BAO measurement under the Overview convention, entered as a fixed-amplitude noise-shaped template with no nuisance freedom; per-channel amplitude marginalisation and down-weighting are outside the forecast and outside this ruling."
- Drop or qualify "Nothing in the table rests on a credit the forecast cannot represent": "except the sidereal-day cap, which is the ground-filter credit for the component constant over more than one day, taken by rule on every row."
- One paragraph on what CHIME's published analyses already do with 470 to 608 MHz, with the citation (the 2023 stacking detection used 587.5 to 800 MHz; verify), so the reader sees that on channels 14 to 32 the ruling quantifies an exclusion already practised and that the genuinely new territory is 33 to 36, where on the east-west basis 33 and 35 are robust and 34 and 36 are at the floor (S1).
- One sentence stating that the excision is a coherence statement: "No channel exceeds tolerance on per-frame power; every excision is the statement that the residual is a bias that does not average down over an integration in which it is not removed."

## 3. Verdict for the author

The claim as the collaboration will hear it, that no policy short of subtraction can save any of twenty-two channels and a third of the band must go, is not what the evidence supports, and the author's own amendment 7 already says so for the basis. Read on the baselines that carry BAO, with the estimator's floor measured by the no-transmitter reference channel, the picture is this. Five channels are excised on a residual measured well above the floor and robust to every credit tabulated: 30 (37 dB over, one live bin, phasor reverses on 22 m so no sidereal credit is supportable), 31 (33.5 dB, persistence supported by 22 m coherence of 0.76 to 0.96), 33 (33 dB, pending the rescan row), 17 (19 dB, at least 8 dB after the largest credit its 22 m coherence allows) and 35 (14 dB, at least 6 dB after that credit, the thinnest of the five and within the block-versus-exponential model uncertainty of about 3 dB). Those five are 30 MHz, not a third of the band, and the collaboration can be told so with confidence once S1 through S4 are in the text. Channel 22 is the one in between: 3.8 times the reference on 22 m with its persistence and credit unmeasured there; undetermined until a longer east-west capture. Channel 27 keeps on baselines longer than about 10 m by the author's own preview, 29 is marginal at the floor, and 14 is undetermined; their 0.3 m excisions are withdrawn as rulings and kept as worst-case readings. The remaining thirteen (15, 16, 18, 19, 20, 21, 23, 25, 26, 28, 32, 34, 36) read within 1 to 2.4 times the reference channel on the BAO classes: the capture cannot show them within tolerance, because its floor is 16 to 20 dB above the tolerance at the measured persistence, and it cannot show them above it either; they are undetermined at the estimator floor, and calling them excised with a 9 to 21 dB bar is not defensible.
